# INFO-2950-Group-Project
This repository is for the group project of INFO 2950. Our group members are: Jiaxin Liu, Amanda Lu and Xi Lin. 

**Use of Folders**

ideas: the folder for storing ideas.

data collection & raw data: the folder to store the raw data collected.

data cleaning & processing: the folder for data cleaning and processing. 

data analysis & model: the folder for conducting data analysis and develop the model.

final report: the folder for writing the final report.



**Group Contract**

We agree to the following policies for our team. Once agreed to, these policies cannot be changed for the duration of the project.

Decision making: Majority vote.

**Communication**

Meeting location: Collegetown Terrace
Methods: WeChat Group Chat & iMessages
Expectations for response times: within 12 hours/24 hours with explaination

**Meeting times**

After each discussion session
Whenever a meeting is needed
Procedure for missing attendance: we let each other know in advance and follow up after we have time. 

**Balance of responsibilities**

Distribution of work that is agreed by everyone


**Conflict Resolution**

Communicate on the exact violation of the contract, and see what should be changed to avoid violating the contract.



**Availability & Absences**

Other people take on their work, and when they return they do more work to compensate for their absence.
